# Three-Part Blog Series

The complete articles are in [`blog/`](../blog/README.md).

## Part 1: Start With the Assay

Explains guide capture, controls, MOI, replicates, and readout breadth. Uses the three public datasets to show how missing metadata changes the valid analysis.

## Part 2: Choose Methods the Data Can Support

Explains paper-native analysis, pseudobulk, SCEPTRE, PS, GSFA, Mixscape, and pertpy. Separates method eligibility from execution.

## Part 3: Move From Perturbation Results to Disease Evidence

Explains the GWAS Catalog query and the support for APOE, TREM2, INPP5D, and SPI1. Separates disease association from causal and mechanistic validation.

Each article links to primers, source data, code, and result tables. Each article states what is measured, generated, missing, and still required.
