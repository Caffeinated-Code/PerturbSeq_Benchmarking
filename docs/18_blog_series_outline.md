# Future Three-Part Blog Series: Editorial Brief

These are publication gates and structure, not draft posts.

## Part 1 — Why Perturb-seq benchmarks need design-aware method routing

Explain guide capture, MOI, controls, donor/replicate structure, targeted versus whole-transcriptome readouts, and applicability. Required evidence: frozen dataset manifest; QC counts; method versions and parameters; completed/skipped/failed status; control calibration. No method ranking without comparable datasets and endpoints.

## Part 2 — From perturbation effects to Alzheimer microglia context

Separate perturbation, expression/state, chromatin, population-genetic, QTL, peak-to-gene, motif/regulon, pathway, and clinical evidence. Required evidence: typed source-level records, genome-build harmonization, cell type and disease state, direction/contrast alignment, independent replication, and tier rationale. Avoid “validated” for database overlap.

## Part 3 — Building an auditable benchmark result product

Show data contracts, provenance, claim traceability, limitations, downloads, and deployment safeguards. Required evidence: contract tests, frozen example release, reproducible build, accessibility review, and a table linking every public claim to result IDs and source records.

Each post should link the two beginner primers, show at least one negative or inconclusive example, disclose missingness, and undergo scientific and statistical review before publication.
